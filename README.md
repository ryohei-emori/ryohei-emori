## Self-introduction

I’m a hands-on practitioner with deep experience across engineering, modeling, analysis, evaluation, and reporting in Machine Learning and Statistics, complemented by a strong consulting mindset and solid software engineering foundations.

I translate ambiguous business problems into well-scoped solutions, design robust system architectures, and deliver production-ready implementations.

With a Master’s degree in Econometrics, I’m passionate about extracting business insights from algorithmic data and human behavior—bridging Econometrics/Statistics with modern ML in real-world products.

**Expertises in**
- Econometrics, Statistics, Machine Learning (applied + research)
- Marketing Science and Finance
- Causal Inference
- Online Advertising Auctions
- Fairness, transportability, and generalizability using observed data
